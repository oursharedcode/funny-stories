# Code of Conduct

Funny Stories is a small project, and the rule is short: **be decent.**

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep criticism about the code, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, PRs, or discussion.

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, and discussions —
and to public conduct that reflects on the project.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving badly, report it privately through GitHub's
**"Report a vulnerability"** style private channel on the repository's
**Security** tab, or by opening an issue that says only "I'd like to raise a
conduct concern privately" — with **no details** — and we'll arrange a private
channel. Reports stay confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions, and may warn or ban anyone
for behavior they judge inappropriate. Decisions are at the maintainers'
discretion.
